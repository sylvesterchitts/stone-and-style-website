How it works

From first message to final fit.

No guesswork and no surprise costs. Here's exactly what happens after you get in touch.

1

Tell us the space

Send rough dimensions or photos on WhatsApp, or drop into the workshop. We'll talk through options and budget.

2

Measure & quote

We template on site so the fit is exact, then quote in writing — materials, edging, fabrication and fitting.

3

Cut & build

Boards are cut and edged, stone is machined and polished, frames are assembled in our Graniteside workshop.

4

Deliver & install

We deliver and fit, seal the joints, align the doors and leave the site clean.
